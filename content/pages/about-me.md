## About

My name is Alexander Christensen, and I work as an IT consultant with a strong focus on modern endpoint management, security, and Microsoft cloud technologies.

For more than a decade, I have helped organizations of all sizes move from traditional on-premises client management to secure, cloud-first platforms built on Microsoft Intune, Windows 365, and the broader Modern Workplace stack.

**I specialise in:**

- Microsoft Intune for Windows, macOS, iOS and Android

- Windows 365 Cloud PCs and virtual device strategies

- Conditional Access and identity-driven security

- Microsoft Defender for Endpoint

- Automation, Autopilot, OSDCloud and client lifecycle processes

- Moving from SCCM to cloud-only management

- Designing secure, scalable endpoint foundations

- Zero Trust, baselines, compliance and hardening

What matters to me is creating solutions that are both technically solid and practical in the real world.

**Most of my work involves:**

- Advising IT leadership

- Designing end-to-end architecture

- Building policies, profiles and automation

- Running migrations and pilots

- Documentation, workshops, and training

- Troubleshooting complex Intune/Security issues

This blog exists because I enjoy sharing real-world experience, deep technical insights, and clear explanations that help others succeed with the Microsoft ecosystem.

If you work with Intune, Windows 365, Conditional Access or Modern Workplace security, I hope you’ll find something useful here.

Feel free to reach out if you want to connect or discuss anything related to modern endpoint management.
