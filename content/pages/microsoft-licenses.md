Using-IT provides Microsoft cloud licenses for business customers, including Microsoft 365, Office 365, Microsoft Intune, Microsoft Defender and related Microsoft cloud services.

Licenses are supplied as recurring subscriptions with monthly or annual billing depending on the selected commitment term.

**Pricing**

Current product pricing is available through our license portal:

**[MineLicenser.dk](http://minelicenser.dk/)**

Pricing may vary depending on Microsoft licensing terms, commitment period, currency adjustments and product changes.

A fixed **DKK 10.00 License Administration fee** is added per invoice.

All prices are excluding VAT unless otherwise stated.

**Payment**

Recurring payments may be collected automatically through MobilePay after the customer has approved a recurring payment agreement.

The payment amount may vary depending on the customer’s active licenses and subscription changes.
